Introduction

Artificial intelligence is transforming modern healthcare. Recent studies have demonstrated that machine learning models can outperform human radiologists in detecting certain types of cancer [1].

Machine learning is a subset of AI. It uses statistical methods to learn from data. Deep learning, a further subset, uses neural networks with many layers.

Methodology

We collected 10,000 medical images from three hospitals. Each image was labeled by two independent radiologists. We then trained a convolutional neural network.

Results

Our model achieved 94.2% accuracy on the test set. This is significantly higher than the baseline of 87.1% (Smith et al., 2020).

Conclusion

AI can improve medical diagnosis. However, ethical concerns remain.
